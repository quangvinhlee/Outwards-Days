Outwards Days makes a little room for outside. Confirm your week, then let Gemma suggest outdoor ideas around your available time and preferences. The scheduler checks the full time budget, and labels basic suggestions when AI cannot produce a valid plan. Open an outing to see its place, preparation, travel, activity, and return time. Optional mini missions give you something to notice or try. Explore nearby parks and mapped routes, then choose what fits. This is an emulator walkthrough of a saved plan.
