Checked 11 October 2026 against https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week1-2026-10-05 and its full details returned by DevRelay.

The entry is one published DEV post with `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge`, using the supplied template. List eligible partner categories in the post. It can enter several categories but win once. Writing quality has the highest judging weight. The deadline is Monday 12 October, 17:59 Australia/Melbourne.
